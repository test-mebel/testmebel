Submit an application

Application for mandatory certification

In accordance with paragraph 9 of Resolution No. 502, applications are submitted through the certification information system. In the application, select the product certification body of “TEST MEBEL” LLC (O‘ZAK.MS.0078) as the body.

sertifikat.tris.uzSingle Window (singlewindow.uz)

Documents to prepare

  • product name, HS code (CN FEA) and the regulatory document for the product (GOST, O‘z DSt, TSh);
  • for serial products (1C) — technical description and drawings of the item, set or series;
  • for imported products — invoice or tax invoice, waybill (shipping document), certificate of origin if available;
  • if a trademark (intellectual property object) is used — information confirming the right to it;
  • management system certificate — if available.

If you are unsure which scheme applies, fill in the request form — a specialist will contact you.
