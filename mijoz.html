Clients’ rights and obligations

The applicant has the right

  • to choose one of the schemes established for the product;
  • to receive information on the certification process and time limits;
  • to confidentiality of their information;
  • to file a complaint or appeal against a decision or action of the body;
  • not to be discriminated against for filing a complaint or appeal.

The applicant and certificate holder must

  • comply with certification requirements and provide the necessary documents;
  • provide conditions for sampling, evaluation and periodic assessment;
  • use the certificate and the conformity mark only for certified products;
  • inform the body of changes in product design, composition or technology;
  • stop using advertising materials referring to the certificate when it is suspended or withdrawn;
  • make payment under the contract.
