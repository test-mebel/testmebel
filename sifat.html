Quality policy

The main area of activity of the certification body is to create conditions for providing the population with high-quality and safe products by certifying domestically manufactured or imported products in accordance with its scope of accreditation.

Management and all staff consider it their goal to carry out every certification application with high quality in accordance with the regulatory documents of the national certification system. To this end, a quality management system conforming to O‘z DSt ISO/IEC 17065:2015 has been developed and implemented.

The body guarantees

  • impartiality, objectivity and reliability of certification results in accordance with the impartiality policy;
  • confidentiality of information;
  • high competence of staff;
  • performance of work within the established time limits, with high quality and at a reasonable price;
  • continual improvement of the system to meet the requirements of applicants and other interested parties.

Quality objectives

  • issuing decisions and certificates for 100 % of applications within the time limits set by Resolution No. 502 and PSK-02;
  • no justified complaints or appeals related to impartiality or procedural violations;
  • 100 % fulfilment of internal audit, monitoring and staff training plans;
  • elimination of nonconformities within the time limits set in the corrective action plan;
  • an impartiality review by the Impartiality Committee at least once a year.

The policy is approved by the director of “TEST MEBEL” LLC (PSK-01-A).
