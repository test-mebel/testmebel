Testing

Certification testing is performed in the testing laboratory of “TEST MEBEL” LLC, accredited to O‘z DSt ISO/IEC 17025:2019. Tests outside the laboratory’s scope are subcontracted to an external accredited laboratory (outsourcing). If the applicant objects to testing in the laboratory, another accredited laboratory is engaged. The body’s specialist takes samples on site in the presence of the applicant, identifies them and delivers them to the laboratory.

  • the sampling and identification report is issued in accordance with PSK-09;
  • under scheme 4C, the item is tested by non-destructive methods;
  • the test report undergoes incoming verification before serving as a basis for the decision (PSK-12);
  • after testing, samples are returned or written off under a report.
